## Dashboards
This folder contains interactive Power BI dashboards developed as part of the Kaggle analytics projects. Each dashboard focuses on solving real-world business problems using data visualization, KPI tracking, and analytical insights.
The dashboards demonstrate end-to-end analytics skills including data modeling, metric design, and business storytelling.

## Netflix Content Analysis Dashboard

Tool: Power BI,
Project Folder: Power-BI/Netflix_Analysis/

## Objective:
To analyze Netflix’s content library and uncover trends across genres, countries, ratings, and time.

## Key Metrics:
1. Total Movies: 6,131
2. Total TV Shows: 2,676
3. Total Titles: 8,807

## Insights Delivered:
1. Top 10 genres by number of titles
2. Top countries producing Netflix content
3. Distribution of Movies vs TV Shows
4. Content added trend over time
5. Rating distribution analysis

## Business Use Case:
-- Helps content strategists understand:
1. Popular genres and production regions
2. Growth patterns in streaming content
3. Audience rating segmentation

## Healthcare Patient Flow & Admission Dashboard

Tool: Power BI,
Project Folder: Power-BI/Healthcare_Analysis/

## Objective:
To analyze hospital patient flow, admission rates, wait times, and satisfaction levels.

## Key Metrics:
1. Total Visits: 9,216
2. Total Admissions: 4,612
3. Admission Rate: 50%
4. Avg Wait Time: 35.26 mins
5. Avg Satisfaction Score: 4.99

## Insights Delivered:
1. Admission rate by department
2. Wait time range analysis
3. Hourly visits & satisfaction trend
4. Demographic breakdown (Gender, Race, Age Group)
5. Department-wise performance analysis

## Business Use Case:
-- Supports hospital management in:
1. Optimizing patient flow
2. Reducing wait times
3. Monitoring satisfaction trends
4. Improving department efficiency

## Skills Demonstrated:
1. Power BI Data Modeling
2. KPI Development
3. DAX Calculations
4. Data Visualization & Dashboard Design
5. Business Insight Extraction
