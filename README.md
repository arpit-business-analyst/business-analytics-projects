## Business Intelligence Projects
(Power BI & Tableau)
This branch contains business intelligence projects developed using Power BI and Tableau.
Each project focuses on transforming structured sales datasets into meaningful insights through dashboard design, KPI tracking, and analytical reporting.
The projects demonstrate practical skills in data visualization, business analysis, and insight-driven storytelling.

## Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)
## Overview
This project analyzes sales performance across different dimensions such as time, region, and product categories. The objective is to monitor business performance and identify revenue trends.

## Project Components
## Datasets
Superstore sales dataset
Includes order details, product information, regional data, and revenue metrics

## Excel
1. Structured data validation
2. Basic aggregation and data preparation
3. Ensured consistency before importing into Power BI

## Dashboards
1. Interactive Power BI report
2. KPI cards for Total Sales & Orders
3. Regional sales distribution
4. Category performance comparison
5. Time-based trend analysis

## Key Analysis Performed
1. Overall sales performance tracking
2. Product category comparison
3. Regional revenue contribution
4. Time-series sales trend
